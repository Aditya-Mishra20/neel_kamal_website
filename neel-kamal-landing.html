About Us

The Neel Kamal Legacy

We are passionate photographers dedicated to capturing stunning images — professional, stunning photos tailored to showcase your story.

At Neel Kamal Wedding Stories, we believe every love story is unique — and your wedding photos should reflect that.

For over 50 years, we have blended candid storytelling with elegant portraiture, capturing weddings across India with a warm, authentic, and timeless style.

From the gentle glow of diyas to the vibrant marigold arches, our lens finds the moments that matter most.
